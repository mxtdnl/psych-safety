    The four behaviours, named

    These were never neutral verbs. Each one is a leadership style the survey measured.

    Support

    Supportive leadership

    Concern for team members’ welfare, building mutual support. It affects psychological safety only indirectly — through team climate. In the model you just ran, Support never touched Safety directly. It moved Climate, and Climate pulled Safety after it.
    Indirect effect only · McKinsey, 2021
    Consult

    Consultative leadership

    Asking opinions before deciding, granting autonomy, seeking consensus. It works both directly and through climate — the only behaviour here that does both, and the only one that appeared in all three of your meters.
    Standardised coefficient 0.54 · McKinsey, 2021
    Challenge

    Challenging leadership

    Asking people to re-examine assumptions and exceed what they thought possible. It can strengthen psychological safety — but only where a positive climate already exists. Below the line it did damage. The sequence is the finding.
    Conditional on positive climate · McKinsey, 2021
    Direct

    Authoritative leadership

    Command and control. Detrimental to psychological safety. It bought delivery early and less of it each round after that, while taking climate and safety down with it.
    Detrimental · McKinsey, 2021

    The least-taught skills are among the most predictive: sponsorship — enabling others’ success ahead of your own — appears in 26% of leadership development programmes, situational humility in 36%. Organisations investing substantially in leadership development are 64% more likely to have senior leaders rated as inclusive. McKinsey, 2021.
